Frax Finance is a fractional-algorithmic stablecoin protocol offering FRAX (USD-pegged stablecoin), FPI (Frax Price Index stablecoin pegged to a consumer goods basket), frxETH (liquid ETH staking derivative), and supporting DeFi infrastructure including Fraxswap, Fraxlend, and Fraxtal L2. The protocol operates Algorithmic Market Operations (AMOs) to manage collateral and generate revenue, and exposes public REST APIs for querying token supply, collateral ratios, AMO positions, pool APRs, and frxETH staking analytics.

**Areas:** DeFi · Stablecoins · Fractional-Algorithmic · Ethereum · frxETH · Liquid Staking · AMO · Collateral
